# Third-party sources

The training and inference scripts import the U-Mamba version of nnU-Net v2. Install that dependency from its author repository and retain its Apache-2.0 license and notices. The backbone source is maintained by its authors. [U-Mamba](https://github.com/bowang-lab/U-Mamba)

nnU-Net v2, Mamba, causal-conv1d, PyTorch, SimpleITK, NumPy, SciPy, nibabel, scikit-image and batchgenerators remain subject to their respective licenses. Exact versions used in the completed experiment are recorded in configs/runtime_versions.json.

AirRC annotations are distributed by their authors under CC BY 4.0. Cite the dataset paper and obtain source CT data through the original distribution channels. [AirRC dataset](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06074-6)

PARSE2022 data access and use follow the challenge organizers' participation rules. Obtain the dataset from the organizers or their designated distribution. [PARSE2022](https://parse2022.grand-challenge.org/Dataset/)

The project reports public dataset identifiers and aggregate experiment results. Public model weights are associated with their experiment configuration and source citations in the release.
